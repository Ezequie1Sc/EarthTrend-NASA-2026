Methodology and limitations

NASA POWER (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources) supplies T2M, modeled air temperature at 2 m, in °C. We use the API annual aggregate (YYYY13) and require every requested year. POWER aggregates monthly products into annual products; EarthTrend does not reconstruct an annual value from partial months. Meteorology derives from MERRA-2, approximately 0.5° latitude × 0.625° longitude. Coordinates select a grid cell, not an exact point measurement.

Ordinary least squares fits T = intercept + slope × year. The slope is multiplied by 10 for °C/decade. Period means are arithmetic averages of annual values; differences are B − A. R² describes fit and the two-sided p-value tests zero slope under regression assumptions. Autocorrelation is not corrected and no causal attribution is attempted. For a constant series, slope = 0, p = 1 and R² = 0 are display conventions; variance-based R² is undefined.

The globe defaults to daily NOAA/NCEP–NCAR R1 data (~1.9° Gaussian grid); optional MERRA-2 layers require imported files. A daily map is not the annual POWER series. Colors saturate outside −20 to 40 °C; missing cells are transparent. Interpolation is visual, not additional measured resolution.
